& Painting · Brand Package Summary (v1.1)
Concept identity. Not trademark-cleared.

1. Assumptions (verify these)

Known: a remodeling and paint company in Dallas–Fort Worth. Assumed: residential homeowners; kitchens, baths, additions, whole-home, interior/exterior painting, cabinet refinishing. Services, cities, price tier, and revenue split are still unknown.

Pushback: the name is strong for local search but weak as a memorable brand. Check Texas SOS, domain, and trademark before printing anything. "Total" is a promise: if you subcontract trades, say "we manage every trade."

2. Positioning & Voice

Position: the DFW remodeler that also paints: one crew, one contact, one finished look from rough-in to final coat.
Pillars: one point of contact · written scope and price · clean, on-schedule jobsites.
Tagline (recommended): Built right. Start to finish. Ads: "Remodeling & painting, handled start to finish."

Voice: steady, plainspoken, precise, like a foreman who explains things. Use specifics, dates and ranges; avoid "dream space" talk, unearned superlatives, and fake urgency.
Bio: Kitchen, bath & whole-home remodeling + painting across DFW. One contact, written scope, clean jobsites. Free estimates.

5. Trust & Compliance (verify before claiming)

  • No years, license numbers, review counts or "insured" badges are invented. Texas has no statewide GC license; check TDLR (electrical/HVAC), TSBPE (plumbing), city registrations and permits.
  • Pre-1978 homes: the EPA Renovation, Repair and Painting (RRP) rule requires certified firm and renovator. Advertise it only if you hold it.
  • Put paint specs in writing: brand/line, coats, prep scope, color code, sheen, inclusions. State a warranty only if you'll honor it.
  • Photos: use real before/afters from your jobs. Don't present AI images as completed work (deceptive; FTC/DTPA risk). Label AI concepts as concepts.

6. Website, Social & Paid

Website (mobile-first)

  • Hero: one outcome, one CTA ("Get a written estimate"), sticky tap-to-call.
  • Order: proof → services → process → gallery → areas → FAQ → form.
  • 44px touch targets, focus states, alt text, real labels.
  • Local SEO: service × city pages; Google Business Profile; consistent name/address/phone.

Content pillars

  • Color transformations (7–10 s Reels, color name on screen)
  • Prep is the job
  • Refinish vs replace cabinets
  • Exterior paint in Texas heat
  • Remodel timelines (real ones only)

Hooks to test

  • "Why your exterior paint is peeling in 3 years (and it isn't the paint)."
  • "Refinish or replace your cabinets? The honest math."
  • "What a painter should put in writing before opening a can."
  • "3 things a contractor should put in writing before touching your house."

Paid (Meta)

  • Lead with painting ("free color consult + written estimate"); nurture/retarget with remodel.
  • Radius targeting around your real service area; 3 creatives; run ≥ 2 weeks.
  • Track cost per qualified lead and per booked estimate.
  • Housing is a Meta special ad category: limited targeting.
